Terry Zee

Two decades turning space into feeling.

For more than twenty years, Terry Zee has worked in a single material — light — and the way a room feels when it’s shaped with intention. A fixture of the Dallas electronic music scene, his immersive environments and kinetic installations built a reputation across the art and lighting world.

An icon in Dallas’s LGBTQ+ and bisexual community, Terry has spent his life channeling a raw, eccentric, unapologetic energy into something precise and luminous — emotion and desire carried on waves of light. It’s what makes his work impossible to mistake for anyone else’s.

Today he creates one-of-one commissions for luxury homes, restaurants, hotels, clubs, business, and private collectors. Every piece begins with the space — its architecture, its atmosphere, and the feeling the client wants to create.

No two installations are ever the same.
