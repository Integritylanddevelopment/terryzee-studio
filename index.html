The Studio

Every piece begins with the space — not a template.

For more than two decades, Terry Zee has turned ordinary rooms into unforgettable experiences — known throughout the Dallas electronic-music scene for immersive environments and kinetic light installations.

Today that work takes the form of one-of-one commissions for luxury homes, hospitality, business, and private collectors. No two installations are ever the same.
